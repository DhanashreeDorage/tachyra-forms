WHAT TACHYRA DOES

Built for the visit physicians don’t have time to prepare for.

Whole-list medication check

Up to 25 current medications checked together, in one pass, against the patient’s history and conditions.

Differential diagnosis support

Possible explanations for the patient’s presentation, ranked for the physician to review. Never an autonomous diagnosis.

Unified records

Labs, imaging reports and other records on file combined into one patient view.
