WHAT YOU’LL BE REVIEWING

A pre-visit review of the patient’s whole picture, for you to judge.

Before each visit, Tachyra reviews the patient’s history, every medication, supplement and over-the-counter product, and their lab trends over time. It gives you a short summary to review, edit and approve. Tachyra never diagnoses or prescribes. You make every decision.

Ranked differential

Possible diagnoses ranked by likelihood, with the findings from the chart for and against each one.

Whole-list medication check

Every medication checked together against the patient’s conditions, not just two drugs at a time.

Personal baseline trends

Slow changes measured against the patient’s own baseline, not just population reference ranges.
